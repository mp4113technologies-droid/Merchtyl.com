Take food orders without a second system.

Lottery

Lottery, handled properly.

Scan tickets like any other product, or key in lottery sales and wins right inside the sale. A win is recorded as a win — not a refund, not a discount — so your numbers stay honest.

  • Scan physical tickets at the register
  • Record lottery sold and wins in the same checkout
  • Wins never reduce your taxable sales
  • Sold, wins and net lottery in your daily reports

Every register.
One report.

  • Sales, tax and payments by type
